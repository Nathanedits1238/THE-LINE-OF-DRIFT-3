# The Line Of Drift (Grand Proved It)

The ultimate 3D drift and police pursuit game, ported to Android (Kotlin & Jetpack Compose).

## Features
- **Story Mode**: Follow Luca & Miguel through clandestine missions and high-speed police pursuits across Neo-Tokyo.
- **Career & Open World**: Explore the city, escape police patrols, and build your reputation.
- **Garage & Customization**: Unlock elite vehicles (JDM, Muscle, Super cars) and upgrade engines, handling, nitro, and armor.
- **Multiplayer**: Join or create online lobbies with 4-digit room codes.
- **Challenges & Leaderboard**: Test your driving skills in extreme drift and speed challenges.
- **Settings & Controls**: Customize audio volume, graphics quality, and touch/gyro controls.
